Delete your Paymentory account

Paymentory · developer contact: fasnexis@gmail.com

You can delete your Paymentory account and everything stored with it at any time, right in the app. If you no longer have the app, you can ask us by email instead.

Option 1: In the app (instant)

  1. Open Paymentory and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all data stored with it are deleted immediately, and the data on that phone is erased.

Option 2: By email (without the app)

  1. Send an email to fasnexis@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Paymentory account”.
  2. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to Paymentory, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account and reply to confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, sign-in details and your profile name
  • Your profile photo
  • Backed-up subscriptions, payments, budgets, savings goals and settings
  • Households you own, and your membership in any other household
  • Your Pro status record and assistant usage count
  • Messages and assistant reports you sent us from the app

What may remain for a short time: deleted data can stay in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a short period before it is overwritten. Purchase records for Pro are kept by Google Play under Google’s own policies, and a Pro subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

Using Paymentory without an account?

In guest mode nothing is stored on our servers. To remove your data, delete it in the app or uninstall Paymentory from your phone.
